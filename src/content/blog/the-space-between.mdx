# The Space Between

Not every silence is empty.

Some silences are crowded with answers that have not yet found the right shape. Others are simply a refusal to respond too quickly.

We tend to treat pauses as defects in a process: a delay before the message, the design, the decision, or the next version. But the pause can be part of the work itself.

## Unfinished is not useless

An unfinished thought can be more honest than a finished sentence delivered too soon. It leaves a door open for context, contradiction, and change.

There is a particular kind of confidence in saying:

> I do not know yet, but I am still looking.

This is not indecision. It is attention extended over time.

## Making room

A page needs margins. A song needs rests. A conversation needs enough air for someone to change their mind.

The same may be true of a life. We do not need to fill every interval just because it is available.

Sometimes the most useful thing we can create is a little space between one certainty and the next.
